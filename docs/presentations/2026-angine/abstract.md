## Analyzing Musical Instruments as Music Theory

The study of musical instruments, often referred to as "organology," is most typically the domain of music historians; however, music theorists are increasingly turning their attention to musical instruments, especially as a way of discussing new repertoire and perspectives. In particular, music theorists seem drawn to the theory of instrumental affordances: the study of what different instruments encourage, discourage, or prohibit outright, and what role that has in music composition and performance.

This talk presents some analytical vignettes that connect close music analysis to instrumental affordances. I discuss examples from the video game _Sonic the Hedgehog 3_ (1994), Angine de Poitrine's album _Vol. II_ (2026), and an improvised performance by Jeff Mills using nothing but a Roland TR-909 drum machine. These examples together demonstrate how centering musical instruments can reveal new research questions in music analysis.
